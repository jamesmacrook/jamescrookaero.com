About

Eastbourne → Cambridge → Oxfordshire → California

I grew up in Eastbourne and started the way a lot of engineers do, building a Locost sports car. Formula Student and an engineering degree at Cambridge took me into Formula 1, where I spent about ten years in Oxfordshire working on aerodynamics with Lotus and then Williams.

In 2022 I moved to Tesla as Staff Aerodynamicist and led aerodynamic development of the next-generation Roadster, including its deployable active rear wing, on which I'm a named inventor.

Now I work with Navier AI on fast, AI-driven aerodynamic simulation, develop O-Wing, a blown-wing propulsion concept for VTOL aircraft, and help early-stage hardware teams with their aero: aircraft, boats, bikes, cars and more.

Tesla · 2022–2026 · Staff Aerodynamicist

Tesla Roadster

I led aerodynamic development of the next-generation Roadster, revealed in October 2026. Its rear wing hides flush in the deck lid for road driving, then a four-bar linkage lifts it up and back into clean air as a slat-and-main-element wing. A controller sets the angle from speed, braking and steering, and a low-drag position flattens it out on the straights.

  • Peak downforce from the wing700 kg
  • Deploy time< 3 s
  • Angle change< 0.25 s
  • Drag removed in low-drag position250 counts

Founder · O-Wing

O-Wing

A blown-wing propulsion concept for vertical take-off. A thin, fast jet bends around a rounded trailing edge and drags the surrounding air with it, giving a lot of lift at very low forward speed. Independent fan units feed one common plenum so the wing tolerates the loss of a unit.
